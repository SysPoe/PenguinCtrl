Extra Cue Types

Group Fade
Fade every active cue matching a label, act, or prefix.

Snapshot
Capture current active volumes and restore them later.

Wait
Delay the next cue, then auto-fire it after a fixed time.

Marker
Operator-only notes that do not execute anything.

Playlist
Fire several sound cues as a sequence or shuffled bed.

MIDI
Send a MIDI note or control change to external software.

HTTP
Call a webhook for projectors, signage, or automation.

Safety Stop
Stop or fade all non-protected cues immediately.
